How Thick Should a Concrete Driveway Be In NZ?

In New Zealand, the thickness of a concrete driveway should be at least 100mm (10cm). This is the minimum thickness that should be used for exposed concrete surfaces. If you are laying concrete over an existing driveway, then the thickness may need to be increased to account for any irregularities in the surface. However, as a general rule, 100mm is the minimum thickness that should be used for a concrete driveway in New Zealand. With this in mind, it is essential to ensure that your contractor is aware of this requirement and is able to meet it. If they are not, then you may want to consider finding another contractor who can do a better job.

Is It Cheaper To Pave or Concrete NZ?

With Concrete Driveway Christchurch – There are a few things to consider when deciding if it is cheaper to pave or concrete your existing driveway. The first is the cost of the materials. Pavers generally cost less than concrete, but the price can vary considerably depending on the type of pavers you choose.

The second is the cost of installation. A DIYer can lay pavers with some basic knowledge and tools, but concrete is much more challenging to install and generally requires the help of a professional. The third factor is maintenance. Pavers may need to be resealed every few years to prevent weeds and moss from growing, but concrete is much more low-maintenance. Overall, deciding whether to pave or concrete your driveway depends on personal preference and budget.

Concrete is the way to go if you want a fantastic job that will last for many years with minimal maintenance. However, paving is a great option if you’re on a tight budget or want a project you can do yourself.

Apart from the above two reasons, it also depends on the size, shape, and layout of your desired concrete driveway. The price range will also depend on the quality of concrete used and any extra features or customisations you may want.
