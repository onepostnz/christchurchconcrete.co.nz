How Much Does Concrete Cost per Sqm?

The cost of concrete per square meter can vary depending on several factors, including the type of concrete, the thickness of the concrete layer, the size of the area, coloured concrete, and whether it is a commercial or residential project. For example, a concrete retaining wall might cost more than a concrete driveway, and the same is true if you need plain concrete or coloured concrete on your driveway.

In general, concrete is priced by square metres. However, some suppliers may also charge by cubic metre, so it is essential to clarify this before ordering.

How Thick Should a Concrete Driveway Be In NZ?

When it comes to concrete driveways, there is no one-size-fits-all answer. The thickness of a concrete driveway depends on various factors, including the intended use, the climate and the soil conditions. For example, a commercial concrete driveway will need to be thicker than a plain concrete driveway to withstand heavy traffic.

In addition, concrete driveways in colder climates will require a thicker layer of concrete to prevent cracking due to freezing and thawing. Ultimately, the best way to determine the thickness of your concrete driveway is to consult with a professional who can assess your specific needs. By considering all of these factors, you can ensure that your concrete driveway has a solid foundation that will last for years to come.
